Evidence behind the prediction

Exact duplicates collapse before splitting. Audited visual groups stay together. The 945 test images belong to 70 groups; match and player identities are unavailable.

Method, scope and limitations +

A frozen MobileNetV2 extracts two feature vectors. The classifier combines them with their absolute difference. Only the linear cricket classifiers are trained. The fixed crop describes a region; it does not detect or segment the batter. A HOG baseline compares grayscale gradients on the same image split. The contrast feature is a modest course-level extension of established methods.

The review rule requires a high enough model score and agreement between the two single-view heads. It is a secondary diagnostic. Test calibration worsened after temperature scaling (ECE 0.043 to 0.094). A model score is not a guarantee of correctness or an unknown-class detector.

The dataset has no verified match IDs. Closely related frames may remain across splits despite visual grouping. The separate eight-class video pilot performs much worse and is available through the link above.
