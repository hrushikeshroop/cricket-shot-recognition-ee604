How it works, and where it can fail +

A fixed crop emphasizes the upper center of each broadcast frame. A frozen MobileNetV2 encoder and a small classifier use ordered phase averages, late-minus-early differences and temporal variation. The strip shows these crops.

A validation-fitted temperature adjusts confidence. Low scores request review. This rule cannot reliably detect non-cricket footage or unknown shot classes, and a confident prediction can still be wrong.

Eight labels come from a small, imbalanced archive. The crop revision followed a failed full-frame pilot, so the reported scores are exploratory results on a reused holdout. Different camera layouts may place the batter outside the crop.
